FAQ

Questions, answered.

Isn't programmatic just retargeting?+

No. We run it as a full-funnel channel. It reaches net-new named accounts and builds your retargeting pool in the first place, then keeps the whole buying committee seeing one story across the web.

How do you measure programmatic if it's not last-click?+

On reach and awareness lift, engagement-signal lift through DemandSense, named accounts moving toward a decision, and the incremental lift the channel adds. Measuring a reach channel on last-click hides where it earns its keep.

Where will my ads actually appear?+

Across premium business and industry publications, news, native and video placements, connected TV, and emerging AI surfaces. We lead with quality of placement.

Do I need a big budget or a lot of conversions?+

No. Our approach is built for B2B, including lower-traffic and lower-conversion accounts, by reading engagement signals through DemandSense rather than relying on high conversion volume.

How is your targeting different from other programmatic or ABM platforms?+

We build your audience from your own first-party signal rather than renting modeled third-party data that decays, and we reach it across the open web. We can also target more granularly by industry than the social platforms allow.

Can you reach people at a specific event or conference?+

Yes, through geofencing. It's one of the most precise, highest-intent plays programmatic offers.
