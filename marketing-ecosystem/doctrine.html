What are the five pillars of a B2B marketing ecosystem?

Every motion belongs to one of five pillars. Pillar names are presentation; the motions and levels are the durable core.

CreateCreate demand and trust before anyone is in-market.
CaptureCatch the roughly 5 percent actively searching.
ConnectTurn anonymous attention into named, scored, routable accounts.
ConvertTurn warm accounts into conversations and pipeline.
CompoundMeasure, diagnose, experiment, reallocate; the system gets smarter every quarter.

How is each motion graded?

One grading scale applies to every motion. Evidence beats claims: a motion is graded on what is actually running, not what is intended.

L0 Absent

Not running.

L1 Foundational

Minimum viable depth, doing its job at small scale.

L2 Operational

Systematic, segmented, measured. The standard for a scaling program.

L3 Compounding

Feeding other motions, running on triggers, self-improving.

Which audiences get budget first?

Budget and effort follow warmth. A colder tier earns budget only when warmer tiers are both fully activated and either saturated or too small. The ladder is dynamic: as warm pools grow, budget continuously re-weights toward them.

A1First-party warm: CRM contacts, past leads, owned lists, website visitors, top-engaged ad accounts.
A2In-market search traffic.
A3Competitor conquest traffic.
A4Signal-built lists of fit accounts (hiring, technographic, growth, granular fit, timing).
A5Broad native channel targeting.

What comes first? The six laws of progression.

Sequencing is not taste. These six laws decide what a company does next, and they are why the Next 3 Unlocks are earned, not sold.

  1. Warm before cold. Unactivated first-party data is always the first recommendation, and it costs nothing.
  2. Foundations before amplification. A message is proven on organic before paid amplifies it. Tracking goes in before spend scales. A landing page exists before conversion offers run to it.
  3. Default build order, client-adjusted entry. The canonical sequence runs Create and thought leadership, then search, then LinkedIn retargeting (which opens the signal layer), then the Connect pillar, then conversion and offers, then routed reach. Enter wherever the client already is; never restart a client at zero.
  4. Saturation triggers segmentation, then expansion. A pool saturating means segment deeper. A reachability map showing unreached ICP accounts means enrich and route them.
  5. Success opens pockets. A validated campaign, audience and offer proven, unlocks activating its unexposed accounts on other channels. Expansion is earned by a working campaign, never proposed in a vacuum.
  6. One experiment per quarter. There is always one bet running with a hypothesis, a budget cap, and a kill or scale criterion.

How is the Ecosystem Maturity Score calculated?

A weighted sum out of 100. Each motion contributes its level divided by 3, times its weight. Transparency is doctrine, so the weights are published here.

Pillar and motion weights

  • Create 25 · C1-C4 at 6.25
  • Connect 25 · C11-C14 at 6.25
  • Convert 20 · C8-C10, C18-C20 at 3.33
  • Capture 15 · C5-C7 at 5
  • Compound 15 · C21-C22 at 7.5
  • Meta / programmatic / email 0 until unlocked

Stage mapping

  • Validate 0 to 20
  • Ramp 21 to 40
  • Reach 41 to 60
  • Supply 61 to 80
  • Sustain 81 to 100

Routed-reach motions (Meta, programmatic, email) carry no score until their Connect-pillar gate opens, then fold into the Convert weight. Where the weighting nuance would confuse an output, the score falls back to equal weight per motion and is labeled as such. Two differently weighted scores are never shown for the same client without a label.

Frequently asked

What does a fully mature B2B marketing ecosystem include?

Twenty-two motions across five pillars: Create, Capture, Connect, Convert and Compound. Each is graded L0 (absent) to L3 (compounding). Maturity is not more channels; it is each motion feeding the next, warm audiences activated before cold, and measurement under everything.

How is B2B marketing maturity scored?

A weighted sum out of 100. Pillar weights are Create 25, Connect 25, Convert 20, Capture 15, Compound 15, spread evenly across the motions in each pillar. Scores map to five stages from Validate (0 to 20) through Sustain (81 to 100).

What should a B2B company fix first?

Warm before cold. Unactivated first-party data is the first move and costs nothing. Then foundations before amplification: message proven on organic, tracking before spend, a page before the offer. New channels are earned by saturation or a proven campaign, never proposed in a vacuum.

When is a company ready for Meta or programmatic?

Meta B2B is never run before enrichment reaches L2, because Meta without enriched audiences is broad consumer spray. Programmatic needs named-account reach at scale with LinkedIn and Google already optimized. Both are routed-reach layers that need the Connect pillar to tell them which accounts to reach.
