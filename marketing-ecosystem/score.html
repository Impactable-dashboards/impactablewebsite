How the scoring works

Every motion is graded L0 (absent) to L3 (compounding) against published definitions. Your Ecosystem Score is a weighted sum out of 100: Create 25, Connect 25, Convert 20, Capture 15, Compound 15, spread evenly across the motions in each pillar. Meta and programmatic carry no score until enrichment reaches L2; once that gate opens they fold into the Convert weight. Stages map as Validate 0 to 20, Ramp 21 to 40, Reach 41 to 60, Supply 61 to 80, Sustain 81 to 100.

The Next 3 Unlocks follow the six laws of progression: warm audiences before cold, foundations before amplification, and expansion earned by saturation or success, never proposed in a vacuum.
