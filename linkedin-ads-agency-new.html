Common questions

Before you book.

What does a B2B LinkedIn ads agency cost?+

Management is a flat monthly fee priced to the operation, not a percent of your budget, so the number holds whether you spend $5k or $200k a month. Managed engagements start at $3,000/mo Core and move up as scope earns it. You can also start with a LinkedIn Ads Launch Plan for $1,500, or a free Demand Plan.

What makes a good LinkedIn ads agency?+

One that does the strategic work before it spends: builds the targeting from real signal, paces warm-first, and reports on named accounts and pipeline instead of impressions. Ask any agency to show you account-level, pipeline-connected reporting. Most cannot.

Do you only run LinkedIn ads?+

LinkedIn is the hub, not the whole system. We run search, programmatic, B2B Facebook, email, and outbound around it, routed by the same signal layer. Everything is built on LinkedIn as the source of B2B buyer intelligence.

How is Impactable different from other LinkedIn ads agencies?+

We're a first-cohort LinkedIn Marketing Partner, LinkedIn published a client's 6X result, and we run on DemandSense, a signal platform we're a top partner in, that connects ad exposure to named accounts and revenue. We build the targeting from real signal before we spend, rather than guessing at an ICP.

How soon will we see results?+

B2B cycles are long, so we don't promise overnight leads. Expect early signal within the first few weeks and pipeline building over a quarter as the data sharpens the targeting.
